# 评论区中英文 FPV 提示词｜可替换模板示例

建议引导语：六种 FPV 运镜的中英文模板在这里。把【括号】里的内容换成你的场景；英文版替换 [brackets]。每次先生成一种动作，中文或英文任选一版，不用两版一起粘贴。

通用前缀：以参考图为起始画面，第一人称 FPV 飞行视角，连续单镜头；保留原图画风、色彩和地标结构，不出现画框、无人机或螺旋桨。

English prefix: Begin from the reference image in a first-person FPV flying view, in one continuous shot. Preserve the image style, colors and landmark geometry. No picture frame, drone body or propellers.

使用提醒：原图里要有对应的高度、通道、开口或主体；先设好 16:9 和生成时长。模板用于明确动作，实际效果仍需检查生成结果。

## 01 俯冲 DIVE

中文：从【高处起点】沿【地标外侧】向下俯冲，视线朝向下方落点；接近【低处终点】时平滑拉平，继续向前飞行。

English: Dive from [high starting point] along the outside of [landmark], looking toward the landing area below. Smoothly level out near [lower endpoint] and continue flying forward.

## 02 贴地飞行 PROXIMITY

中文：贴近【地面或表面】沿【通道】低空前进，保持高度和前向视线；近处前景快速后退，远处环境变化较小，结束时继续沿原路飞行。

English: Fly low and close to [ground or surface] along [clear corridor], maintaining altitude and a forward view. Nearby foreground rushes backward while distant scenery changes more slowly. End continuing along the same path.

## 03 环绕 ORBIT

中文：从【主体正面】开始，以稳定高度和距离沿弧线环绕【主体】半圈；镜头始终看向主体中心，主体保持居中，背景产生视差，结束于主体背面。

English: Start in front of [subject] and orbit it through a half-circle at a stable height and distance. Keep looking at the subject center so it stays centered while the background shifts with parallax. End behind the subject.

## 04 钻越 GAP

中文：对准【可通行开口】中心连续前进，门框逐渐扩大并掠过两侧；穿过开口后显露【出口景观】，结束于开阔空间，不穿实体墙面，不切镜头。

English: Fly continuously toward the center of [passable opening]. Its edges enlarge and sweep past both sides of the frame. Pass through the opening to reveal [view beyond], ending in open space without crossing solid walls or cutting.

## 05 追踪 TRACKING

中文：从【移动主体】后方沿【路线】持续追踪，以相近速度保持相对距离；主体居中、大小稳定，环境持续后退，结束时仍在跟随，不超车。

English: Track [moving subject] from behind along [route], matching speed to maintain relative distance. Keep the subject centered at a stable size as the environment flows backward. End still following without overtaking.

## 06 翻滚 ROLL

中文：沿【开阔通道】向前飞行，绕前进轴顺时针完成一次 360 度滚转；地平线连续倾斜、倒置、恢复水平，最后保持水平继续前进，场景结构不变。

English: Fly forward along [open corridor] while performing one clockwise 360-degree roll about the forward axis. The horizon continuously tilts, becomes inverted, and returns to level. End flying forward with a level horizon; preserve scene geometry.
